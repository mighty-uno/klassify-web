The problem

School software runs on desktop, but school life runs everywhere.

Traditional ERPs stop at the school gate — teachers and parents are left out of the loop, and admin teams drown in manual work.

Admin

Fees tracked in spreadsheets, reports compiled by hand, reminders chased one by one. No single, live view of the school.

Teacher

Manual roll call eats minutes from every class. Homework and memos are scattered across WhatsApp groups and paper registers.

Parent

Out of the loop on attendance, fees and assignments. Missed payment dates, missed updates, and no easy way to ask.

Our objective

What problems we're solving

We're not porting the web to mobile — we're building the right layer for each role. Web for depth, mobile for speed, AI for answers.

01

Consolidate the chaos

One source of truth for students, fees, attendance, schedules and payroll — replacing spreadsheets and scattered WhatsApp groups.

02

Make admin frictionless

Collection vs target at a glance, drillable charts, defaulters surfaced automatically, and follow-ups handled for you.

03

Give teachers their time back

Roll call in under two minutes, QR self check-in, homework broadcast to students in one tap, a memo after every class.

04

Bring parents in the loop

Live attendance, fee status and school updates on their phone — with one-tap acknowledgement so nothing is missed.

05

Put an AI on every desk

SkooBee AI answers questions from live data, generates reports and sends reminders — instead of menus and manual exports.

06

Reach where life happens

A mobile-first layer so admins, teachers and parents stay connected outside the office — not chained to a desktop browser.
